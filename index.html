Meet Huck

The dog behind the Good Life

Rescued in 2013, this Corgi-Chihuahua mix - yes, you read that right - was pure chaos wrapped in charm. Crazy, lovable, and impossible not to smile at, Huck won over everyone he met.

He lived for treats, belly rubs, and laying by the pool soaking up the Florida sun.

Huck is no longer with us, but he left something behind - the reminder that a good life is always within reach.
